Personal Google Tasks

Personal Google Tasks is a test tool for personal shopping capture. It runs on your computer. It reads your default Google Tasks list.

The tool selects titles that start with "shopping" or "cumpără". It prints the product phrase on your computer. It saves the task ID, then tries to delete the task from Google Tasks.

The tool is not a public service. This website explains the tool and how it uses Google data. The website does not sign you in or read your tasks.

Please read the privacy policy and terms before you grant access to the tool.
